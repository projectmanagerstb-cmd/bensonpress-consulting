LICENSING / DELIVERY

Clear terms.
Usable files.

Need to adapt a kit with your team? That's exactly what the internal-use license is designed for.

What can I do with my license?

Adapt the files for internal use at the licensed level. Individual is one user; Team covers up to 10 users; Enterprise permits internal organization-wide use. Resale or external redistribution is not included.

When do I receive the files?

After payment has been verified, Sean emails the purchased kit files to the buyer, normally within one business day. Delivery is not instantaneous or automated.

Can you customize the kit for our organization?

Yes. Customization, facilitation, implementation, and advisory services are quoted separately. Request a consultation for a scoped engagement.

Do the AI materials certify compliance?

No. They provide practical controls and documentation practices. They are not legal advice or certification of compliance with any law, framework, or industry standard.
