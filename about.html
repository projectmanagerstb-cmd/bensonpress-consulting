Benson Press Consulting is led personally by Sean T. Benson, PMP, Lean Six Sigma Black Belt, and a retired U.S. Marine Corps veteran.

Over more than two decades in information technology and program delivery, Sean has led initiatives and teams in defense, government, healthcare, transportation, cybersecurity, and enterprise technology. His work centers on the intersection of governance, delivery, and organizational leadership.

Sean teaches leadership and project management at National University. His doctoral research focuses on AI governance maturity and AI-supported decision quality in project planning.

He is the author of Establishing A PMO and writes about practical PMO leadership and the application of artificial intelligence in project, program, and portfolio environments through Benson Press.
