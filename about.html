About Sean

Built on experience. Focused on results.

Sean T. Benson is the founder and principal consultant of Benson Press Consulting, a retired United States Marine, project and program management professional, university educator, and author.

His career spans defense, government, healthcare, public transportation, cybersecurity, and enterprise information technology. Across these environments, Sean has led teams and initiatives, strengthened project governance, improved delivery visibility, and connected organizational strategy to disciplined execution.

He brings an operator’s perspective to every engagement: leadership decisions must be clear, accountability must be visible, and management systems must help teams deliver—not slow them down.
