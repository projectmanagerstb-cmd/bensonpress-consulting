HOW THE AUTOMATION WORKS

Six steps from
owner to evidence.

Each step is a sheet in the workbook. Formula cells are locked. Input cells are the ones you edit.

  1. 01

    Define work packages

    List the deliverables and decisions that need an owner, each with a target date and health status.

  2. 02

    Assign RACI owners

    Choose R, A, C, or I for each person. The workbook flags any row without exactly one Accountable and at least one Responsible.

  3. 03

    Route recipients

    The purpose of each message derives its To and Cc lists from the RACI. Recipients are not typed by hand.

  4. 04

    Schedule cadence

    Set each communication to one-time, daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, or milestone. The next due date calculates itself.

  5. 05

    Notify email/alerts

    Overdue and due-today items come up with Outlook-ready recipient lists and a pre-addressed email draft.

  6. 06

    Record audit trail

    Each send and acknowledgment is logged as evidence. The schedule moves forward, and anything past its SLA is flagged.

FAQ

Clear limits.
Usable files.

Does it send emails by itself?

Not out of the box, and that is deliberate. Approvals and escalations deserve a human read before they go out. For routine status and reminders, the optional Power Automate flow can send on a schedule and write its own audit-log entries. Those scripts live in your own Microsoft 365 or Google account, not inside the file.

Can I change the routing rules?

Yes. Each purpose has editable To and Cc role codes, an acknowledgment rule, and an SLA. You can override any single communication, add your own purposes, and test them in the Routing Preview.

Will I break the formulas?

Formula cells are locked and shaded. Input cells are white and unlocked. Named ranges clear the sample data without touching formulas.

What can I do under the license?

Adapt the files for internal use at the licensed level. Individual is one user. Team covers up to 10 users. Enterprise is internal use across the purchasing organization. Resale, sublicensing, and external redistribution are not included.

How do I receive the files?

After purchase, Sean emails the kit files within 1 business day. Delivery is not instant or automated.

Can you set it up for our organization?

Yes. Customization, facilitation, and implementation are quoted separately. Request a consultation for a scoped engagement.

Is this a compliance certification?

No. The kit gives you practical controls and a record of who was told what. It is not legal advice or certification against any standard.
