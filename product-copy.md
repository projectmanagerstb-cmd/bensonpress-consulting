## One-line value proposition

**Define accountability once, and every project message routes itself.** The right people get each update, approval request and escalation on schedule, and you have an audit trail to prove it.

## Short description (kit card / meta description)

An Excel workbook with no macros that turns your RACI matrix into a communication system. It derives recipients from decision rights, schedules every update on its cadence, flags what is overdue, builds Outlook-ready email lists, and logs every send and acknowledgment.

## Who it's for

- **Project and program managers** who are tired of routing approvals and escalations from memory.
- **PMO leads** who need one standard for who is told what, when, and with what evidence.
- **Sponsors and functional leaders** who want decision requests that reach the decision owner the first time.
- **Teams in regulated or audit-heavy environments** (government, healthcare, transportation, technology) where "we told them" has to be provable.

## How the automation works

| Step | | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Define** | Work packages | List the deliverables and decisions that need an owner, each with a target date and health status. |
| **02 Assign** | RACI owners | Choose R, A, C or I for each person; the workbook flags any row without exactly one Accountable and at least one Responsible. |
| **03 Route** | Recipients | The purpose of each message (approval, consultation, escalation…) derives its To and Cc lists straight from the RACI. |
| **04 Schedule** | Cadence | Set each communication to one-time, daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly or milestone, and the next due date calculates itself. |
| **05 Notify** | Email / alerts | Overdue and due-today items come up with Outlook-ready recipient lists and a pre-addressed email draft. |
| **06 Record** | Audit trail | Each send and acknowledgment is logged as evidence, which moves the schedule forward and flags anything past its SLA. |

## FAQs

**Does it work in Excel and Google Sheets?**
Yes. It works in Microsoft Excel 2019, 2021 and Microsoft 365 (Windows, Mac and Excel for the web), in Google Sheets (File › Import), and in LibreOffice Calc 7+. The recipient lists use TEXTJOIN and MAXIFS, so Excel 2016 and earlier are not supported.

**Does it use macros? Will my IT team block it?**
No macros and no VBA. It's a standard .xlsx file. "Notify" works by showing what is due and preparing ready-to-send recipient lists and email drafts, and you choose what to send. If you want hands-off sending, the guide shows an optional Power Automate or Google Apps Script setup. Those scripts live in your own Microsoft 365 or Google account, not inside the file.

**Does it send emails by itself?**
Not out of the box, and that's deliberate. Approvals and escalations deserve a human read before they go out. For routine status and reminders, the optional Power Automate flow can send on schedule and write its own audit-log entries.

**Can I change the routing rules?**
Yes. Each purpose has editable To/Cc role codes (for example AR = Accountable + Responsible), an acknowledgment rule and an SLA. You can override any single communication, add your own purposes, and test everything in the Routing Preview.

**How big a project does it handle?**
16 stakeholders, 100 work packages, 50 communications and 300 log entries per workbook. Sheets are protected without a password, so you can extend them.

**Will I break the formulas?**
Very unlikely. Formula cells are locked and shaded, and input cells are white and unlocked. Named ranges (Clear_RACI, Clear_Log and so on) clear the sample data in seconds without touching formulas.

**What can I do under the license?**

**Is this a compliance certification?**
