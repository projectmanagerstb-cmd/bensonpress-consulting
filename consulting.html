PMO Setup &
Consulting

Stand up a PMO that earns its place, or rebuild one that has lost the confidence of leadership.

Who it's for

PMO directors, executives, and operations leaders launching a project management office, resetting its mandate, or struggling with inconsistent portfolio decisions.

Typical deliverables

  • PMO maturity baseline and gap analysis
  • Charter, mandate, and operating model
  • Intake, prioritization, and stage gates

  • RACI, decision rights, and governance cadence
  • 30/60/90-day rollout and adoption roadmap

How we engage

AI Governance
for PMOs

Make AI use in project, program, and portfolio work visible, bounded, explainable, and owned.

Who it's for

PMOs and enterprise leaders facing ungoverned AI use, tool sprawl, sensitive project data, or pressure to automate decisions without clear accountability.

Typical deliverables

  • AI use-case and tool inventory
  • Risk tiers and escalation thresholds
  • AI delegation ladder (L0–L5)
  • Human Accountability Gate and approval logs
  • Policy, controls, and vendor scorecard
  • Pilot oversight and effectiveness measures

What this is—and isn't

Practical operating governance informed by established risk-management approaches. It is not legal advice, a compliance audit, or a certification of any AI system.
