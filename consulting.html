Services

What we do

Benson Press Consulting works with PMO directors, executives, and operations leaders. Each engagement is led by Sean T. Benson. Delivery is remote or onsite, Southern California and remote. Pricing is by inquiry.

Service

PMO Setup & Consulting

Who it's for

PMO directors, executives, and operations leaders who have been asked to set up a project management office, or to fix one that is not being used.

What's delivered

An assessment of how projects are selected and governed, a charter and operating model, intake and reporting that fit the organization, and a rollout the sponsor can follow. The PMO Setup Kit can be the working set of templates for the engagement, or purchased on its own.

How engagements run

Remote or onsite, Southern California and remote. Pricing by inquiry. An engagement starts when both sides agree the scope in writing. Invoices are paid on the Pay page.

Service

AI Governance for PMOs

Who it's for

PMO directors and executives who need AI already in use on project, program, and portfolio work to be visible, limited, and owned.

What's delivered

A practical governance approach: an inventory of AI use, decision rights, delegation limits, risk tiers, and a named person behind consequential decisions. This is operating guidance for the PMO, not legal advice and not a compliance certification. The AI Governance for PMOs kit holds the policy, workbook, and forms.

How engagements run

Remote or onsite, Southern California and remote. Pricing by inquiry. Sean's doctoral research is on AI governance maturity and AI-supported project-planning decision quality.

Service

Training & Workshops

Who it's for

Teams and leaders who need project management and leadership practice they can use on the job, rather than a lecture.

What's delivered

Workshops sized to the team. The flagship is a two-day Project Management & Leadership Bootcamp (12 instructional hours). Half-day and full-day workshops are also available. Participants leave with a workplace artifact and a 30-day application commitment. This is independent Benson Press training. It is not a PMI course and it does not confer a PMI credential.

How engagements run

Remote or onsite, Southern California and remote. Pricing by inquiry. Cohorts are typically discussed when you book. Invoices are paid on the Pay page.
